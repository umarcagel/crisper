# Crisper Video Compressor

Offline Android video compressor focused on quality. Android 7.0+.

Based on [JoshAtticus/Compressor](https://github.com/JoshAtticus/Compressor) (MIT License, see LICENSE).
Roadmap: presets (Small/Medium/Best), Ultra mode for WhatsApp Status, size estimate, batch, share in/out, metadata, compare preview.
